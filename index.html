Stages of Work

1.
Initial Consultation & Order Request
We begin by exploring your vision, functional needs, and aesthetic preferences to understand the full scope of your project. During this stage, we clarify style direction, materials, and budget expectations, forming a clear foundation for the upcoming work.
2.
On-Site Visit & Measurements (optional)
A specialist arrives on-site to take precise measurements and assess the room’s layout, lighting, and architectural features. This step helps us tailor the design to your exact space, ensuring ideal proportions, functionality, and seamless integration with your interior.
3.
Concept Development & Design Project
Our team develops a detailed design concept based on your requirements, offering 2D or 3D visualizations to help you envision the final result. We carefully select materials, finishes, and hardware, ensuring that every design element reflects the style and atmosphere you desire.
4.
Approval & Contract Agreement
Once the concept is refined, we finalize all design details, confirm the production timeline, and prepare a transparent cost estimate. After your approval, we formalize the agreement, ensuring mutual clarity, structured planning, and smooth coordination throughout the entire process.
5.
Production & Delivery
Your project moves into the manufacturing stage, where skilled craftsmen follow strict quality standards to ensure flawless results. Each component is carefully prepared and inspected before secure packaging. After production, we coordinate safe and timely delivery directly to your location.
6.
Installation & Post-Installation Support (optional)
Our team provides professional installation, ensuring precise assembly and perfect alignment of every element. After completion, we conduct functional checks and offer ongoing support, including warranty service and maintenance, ensuring lasting comfort and satisfaction with your new interior.
